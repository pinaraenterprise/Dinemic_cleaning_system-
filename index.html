Electrical Engineering

Comprehensive Electrical Solutions

From heavy-duty industrial connections to clean domestic wiring, our technical approach prioritizes safety standards and long-term durability.

  • ⚡ Industrial Electrical Work: Machine connections, factory cabling, distribution setups, and load management.
  • ⚡ House & Shop Wiring: Complete new electrical construction, conduit runs, and switchboard modernizations.
  • ⚡ Electrical Fault Finding: Rapid isolation and repair of short circuits, breaker trips, and voltage irregularities.
  • ⚡ Control Panel Work: Starter panels, contactor setups, relay replacements, and safety breaker testing.
  • ⚡ Motor & Pump Diagnostics: Phase inspections, capacitor tests, insulation resistance checks, and connection repairs.
  • ⚡ Scheduled Maintenance: Busbar inspection, thermal diagnostics, earthing resistance audits, and load balancing.
  • ⚡ Installation & Repair: Upgrades for modern industrial fixtures, MCBs, MCCBs, and DB panels.
